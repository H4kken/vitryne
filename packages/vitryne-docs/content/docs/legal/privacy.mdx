This is the privacy policy of **vitryne.app**, the hosted Vitryne run by Mathis Grimberg
(contact: mathis.grimberg@protonmail.com), who is responsible for the data described here. Someone who runs their own
instance publishes their own policy.

## Without the cloud, nothing leaves your browser

Vitryne runs in your browser. Opening examples, importing patterns, designing and exporting send
nothing to us. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookie. While you work, an
unsaved copy of your project is kept in your browser's storage so a crash loses nothing; it never
leaves your device.

## When you save to the cloud

Projects are saved to the cloud as you work. We store:

| What                                                                                                                                | Why                                                                                                                         | How long                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your projects (pattern pieces, seams, fabrics, avatar, names)                                                                       | To keep them for you and open them on your devices                                                                          | Until you delete them or your account; without an account, 30 days after your last visit |
| An account identifier, created the first time you save                                                                              | To know which projects are yours                                                                                            | Until you delete your account; without an account, 30 days after your last visit         |
| Your email address and a password (stored only as a hash), or, if you sign in with Google, the email address and name Google shares | To let you sign in and open your projects elsewhere, and to send the links that confirm your address or reset your password | Until you delete your account                                                            |
| Sign-in records: when you signed in, and the IP address used                                                                        | To keep accounts secure and limit abuse                                                                                     | As long as the account exists                                                            |

Your browser keeps your sign-in session in its local storage, which the cloud features you asked
for need.

We use this data only to provide cloud storage to you (the legal basis is our agreement with you,
GDPR art. 6(1)(b); for the sign-in records, our legitimate interest in security, art. 6(1)(f)). We
don't sell it, don't use it for advertising, and don't count or profile visitors.

## Where it is stored, and who else handles it

The data is stored by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, on servers in Paris, France.
Supabase processes it on our behalf. The emails are sent through an email provider that handles
your address for that purpose only. If you choose **Continue with Google**, Google tells us your
email address and name; we get nothing else from your Google account, and what Google itself does is
covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your rights

- **See and take your data:** in the editor, open _Cloud_, then _Download all my data_: every
  project as a file, and what your account holds.
- **Delete it:** _Cloud_, then _Delete my account_, removes your account, your projects and their
  files at once.
- **Correct it, restrict or object to its use:** write to mathis.grimberg@protonmail.com.
- **Complain:** you can contact your data protection authority (in France, the
  [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## Open source

Vitryne is free software (AGPL-3.0-or-later): anyone can read exactly what it does, or run their own
instance. What you design with it is yours.
