A fabric in Vitryne has two sides: **how it hangs** (its kind: light jersey falls close to the
body, denim stands away in folds) and **how it looks** (its colour). Every project starts with
one fabric, cotton jersey, used by the whole garment.

## Make a fabric

1. Open **Fabric** in the bar on the left. It lists the project's fabrics; the one marked
   **Default** is the garment's.
2. Choose **New fabric**.
3. Pick a **Kind**:

   | Kind                  | Weight   | Good for                           |
   | --------------------- | -------- | ---------------------------------- |
   | **Cotton jersey**     | 150 g/m² | T-shirts: soft, follows the body   |
   | **Denim**             | 420 g/m² | Jeans: heavy, stands away in folds |
   | **Poplin (shirting)** | 125 g/m² | Shirts: light, crisp small folds   |
   | **Fleece**            | 280 g/m² | Sweatshirts: thick, rounded folds  |
   | **Wool suiting**      | 250 g/m² | Tailoring: fluid, clean creases    |

4. Choose a **Colour** and a **Name**, then **Make this fabric**.

The kinds behave like the real fabrics: their weights, stretch and stiffness come from
measurements of real cloth.

## Use it for the whole garment

Open the new fabric in the **Fabric** panel and choose **Use it for the whole garment**. The
garment drapes again in the new fabric: every piece that hasn't got a fabric of its own wears it.

## Use it for one piece

A garment can mix fabrics: a fleece body with jersey cuffs, a shirt with a contrasting collar.

1. Click the piece, then open **Pieces**.
2. Open the fabric list under the piece's name and pick a fabric. **Garment's** follows the
   garment's fabric; **New fabric…** makes one for this piece straight away.

A pair of pieces (a left and a right sleeve) always shares one fabric.

## Fine-tune

Open a fabric and unfold **Fine tune** to change it beyond its kind. Each slider says which way
is which:

| Setting                        | From     | To    |
| ------------------------------ | -------- | ----- |
| **Weight**                     | Light    | Heavy |
| **Thickness**                  | Thin     | Thick |
| **Stretch along the grain**    | Stretchy | Firm  |
| **Stretch across the grain**   | Stretchy | Firm  |
| **Stretch on the bias**        | Stretchy | Firm  |
| **Stiffness along the grain**  | Drapey   | Stiff |
| **Stiffness across the grain** | Drapey   | Stiff |
| **Grip on the body**           | Slides   | Grips |

_Along the grain_ is the direction of the piece's grain line (see
[Add marks](/guide/first-pattern#add-marks)), _across_ is at a right angle to it, and _the bias_
is the diagonal. A fine-tuned fabric shows **Custom (fine tuned)** as its kind; picking a kind
again starts over from that kind.

Changes to how a fabric hangs drape the garment again; a colour change shows at once.

## Reuse fabrics from other projects

With a free account, **From my projects** in the **Fabric** panel lists the fabrics of your other
projects; **Add** copies one into this project.

## Delete a fabric

A fabric that no piece uses and isn't the garment's can be removed: open it and choose **Delete
this fabric**.
