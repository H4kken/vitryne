The editor shows your garment twice: as flat pieces of fabric on the left (the **2D pattern**)
and on a body on the right (the **3D drape**). Change one and the other follows.

![The Vitryne editor: the top bar, the panel bar on the left, the 2D pattern with its tool bar, and the 3D drape with its own tool bar.](/editor-overview.png)

The screen has five parts:

1. The **top bar**, across the top.
2. The **panel bar**, down the left side: Fabric, Pieces and Settings.
3. The **2D pattern**, with its tool bar at the bottom.
4. The **3D drape**, with its own tool bar at the bottom.
5. The **divider** between the two views.

## The top bar

On the left:

- **The logo** takes you back to the start screen, where you can open another project.
- **The project name.** Click it to rename the project. Next to it, the save state: _Saved_ once
  your last change is kept. Your work saves by itself as you go; there's no Save button to
  remember.

On the right, from left to right:

![The right of the top bar: Undo, Redo, the 2D and 3D view buttons, Help, Cloud and Export.](/editor-overview-navbar_right.png)

- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, or ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z on a Mac). Hover Undo to see what
  it will take back.
- **Show 2D pattern** and **Show 3D drape**, the two buttons side by side. Hide one view to give
  the other the whole screen.
- **Help** (the question mark): send feedback, report a bug, or read the source code.
- **Cloud** (or **Sign in**): your account. Without one you can keep a single draft; a free account
  keeps as many projects as you like.
- **Export** saves the garment as a 3D model. See [Exports](/imports_exports/exports).

## The panel bar

The three buttons on the left each open a panel beside the pattern. Click a button again to
close its panel.

![The panel bar: Fabric, Pieces and Settings.](/editor-overview-left_panel.png)

| Panel        | What it holds                                                                                         |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fabric**   | The fabric the garment is made of: denim, jersey, wool… See [Your first Fabric](/guide/first-fabric). |
| **Pieces**   | The selected piece: its name, where it starts on the body, its fabric, and the seams.                 |
| **Settings** | The project's name and the drape's **Detail**: more detail drapes finer folds, but slower.            |

## The 2D pattern

The flat view of your pieces, on a grid of centimetres. The scale bar in the bottom right shows
how long 10 cm is at the current zoom.

A new project starts empty, with a card offering three ways to begin: **Draw** a piece with the
pen, start from a **Shape** with typed sizes, or **Import** pieces from a DXF pattern (see
[Imports](/imports_exports/imports)).

![The 2D pattern of a new project: the grid, the Draw your first piece card, the tool bar at the bottom and the scale bar.](/editor-overview-2Dwindow.png)

The tool bar at the bottom, from left to right:

| Tool                                | What it does                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Select**                          | Click a piece to select it, drag it to move it. Shift-click adds more. |
| **Edit points**                     | Move a piece's corners, change an edge's length, curve an edge.        |
| **Draw a piece**                    | The pen: click corner after corner to draw an outline.                 |
| **Lines, notches, folds and grain** | The marks a pattern carries.                                           |
| **Add a shape**                     | A ready shape, such as a rectangle, sized as you drag.                 |
| **Sew two edges**                   | Click an edge of one piece, then the edge it joins.                    |

[Your first Pattern](/guide/first-pattern) walks through each of them.

### Moving around in 2D

- **Zoom**: scroll, or drag two fingers up and down (or pinch) on a trackpad. It zooms around the pointer.
- **Pan**: hold Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) and drag.
- With **Select**, dragging on empty space draws a box that selects every piece it touches.
- **Delete** (or Backspace) removes the selected pieces.

## The 3D drape

Your pieces on the body, falling and settling like real fabric. The label in the top left says
what the drape is doing: _No pieces yet_, _Fabric is settling live_, _Paused_…

![The 3D drape: the body, the status label in the top left, and the tool bar at the bottom.](/editor-overview-3Dwindow.png)

The tool bar at the bottom has two groups. The first is what a click or a drag does in the view:

- **Select**: click a piece to select it; handles let you move it up, down and around the body.
- **Pinch fabric**: grab the cloth and pull it, to check how it moves.

The second controls the drape itself:

| Button                         | Key   | What it does                                      |
| ------------------------------ | ----- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Play / Pause**               | Space | Stops the fabric where it is, or lets it fall on. |
| **Fast drape**                 | F     | Gets to the result sooner.                        |
| **Drape again from the start** | R     | Drops the pieces again from where they start.     |

The drape plays by itself after every change you make, in 2D or 3D.

### Moving around in 3D

- **Zoom**: scroll, or drag two fingers up and down (or pinch) on a trackpad.
- **Turn**: hold **⌘** (Ctrl) and drag. Dragging empty space turns the view too.
- **Slide**: hold **⌥** (Alt) and drag to move the view up, down and sideways.

## The divider

The thin bar between the two views sets how much room each one gets:

- **Drag** it to resize the views.
- **Click** it to step between a few ready splits.
- **Double-click** it to split the screen in half.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys                          | What they do               |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Ctrl+Z / ⌘Z                   | Undo                       |
| Ctrl+Shift+Z / ⌘⇧Z, or Ctrl+Y | Redo                       |
| Delete or Backspace           | Remove the selected pieces |
| Space                         | Play or pause the drape    |
| F                             | Fast drape on or off       |
| R                             | Drape again from the start |
| Ctrl or ⌘, held, and drag     | Pan the 2D pattern         |
