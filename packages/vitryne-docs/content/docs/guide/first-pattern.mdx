By the end of this page you'll have a skirt on the body: two pieces of fabric, a front and a
back, sewn at the sides. It takes about five minutes and teaches the three things every garment
needs: **pieces**, **where they start on the body**, and **seams**.

## Start an empty garment

1. Go to [vitryne.app](https://vitryne.app).
2. Choose **Start from scratch**.

You'll see the body on one side (the **3D drape**) and an empty sheet on the other (the **2D
pattern**), with a card saying _Draw your first piece_. Your work saves by itself as you go.

## Add the front

1. In the tool bar at the bottom of the pattern, pick **Add a shape**, then **Rectangle**.
2. Drag across the sheet. The size shows above the rectangle as you drag: aim for about
   **450 × 400 mm** (450 wide, 400 long), then let go.

The rectangle appears on the body, at the front. It's called _Piece 1_ for now.

<Callout title="Just click?">
  A click without dragging puts down a rectangle of 500 × 700 mm. You can change
  its size afterwards: see [Change a piece's shape](#change-a-pieces-shape).
</Callout>

## Name it and lower it to the waist

New pieces start at the neck. A skirt starts at the waist, so move it down:

1. Click the piece, then open **Pieces** in the bar on the left.
2. Change **Piece name** to `Front`.
3. Under **Where it starts**, lower **Height** to about **1000 mm** (the height above the floor).
   Watch the 3D drape: the top edge should sit just below the waist.

## Add the back

1. Pick **Add a shape** → **Rectangle** again and drag out the same size, next to the front.
2. In **Pieces**, name it `Back`.
3. Set **Where it starts on the body** to **Body, back**.
4. Lower **Height** to the same value as the front.

You now have two pieces hanging around the body, front and back, not joined yet.

## Sew the sides

A seam joins one edge of a piece to an edge of another. Vitryne sews **whole edges**, from
corner to corner.

1. In **Pieces**, under **Seams**, choose **Suggest seams**.
2. Vitryne suggests the pairs of edges that look like they belong together. For each side seam
   (front's side with back's side), choose **Sew**. Choose **Skip** for anything else.

The pieces pull together around the hips: you have a skirt.

<Callout title="Sew by hand">
  To join two edges yourself, pick **Sew two edges** in the pattern's tool bar,
  click an edge of one piece, then the edge of the other piece it should join.
</Callout>

## Watch it drape

The 3D drape plays by itself after every change. Its tool bar has:

- **Play / Pause** (space bar) to stop the fabric where it is.
- **Fast drape** (F) to get to the result sooner.
- **Drape again from the start** (R) to drop the pieces from where they start.
- **Pinch fabric** to grab the cloth and pull it. Hold ⌘ (Ctrl) and drag to look around the body, ⌥ (Alt) and drag to slide the view.

Made a mistake? Undo and redo are in the bar at the top (Ctrl+Z or ⌘Z undoes).

## Draw your own shape

Rectangles get you started; most garments need their own outlines. Pick **Draw a piece** (the
pen):

1. Click to put down each corner. For an exact edge, type its **Length** and **Angle** in the bar
   above the pattern and press Enter.
2. Close the piece by clicking the first point, or with **Close** (Enter). **Undo point**
   (Backspace) takes the last one back; **Cancel** (Esc) stops.
3. Give the piece a name and choose where it goes on the body. **Cut 2 (a pair)** makes two
   copies, the second one the mirror image: a left and a right sleeve, for example.

## Change a piece's shape

Pick **Edit points**, then:

- **Drag a point** to move it.
- **Click an edge** to see its length, type a new one for a straight edge, or turn it into a
  **curve** with two handles to drag.
- **Add point** (or double-click an edge) puts a point in the middle of an edge; **Delete**
  removes a selected point.

## Add marks

**Lines, notches, folds and grain** adds the marks a pattern carries:

| Mark      | What it's for                                                 | How                                                |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Line**  | Pocket placement, darts, stitching lines                      | Click inside a piece; drag for a curve; Enter ends |
| **Notch** | Where two pieces line up when sewing                          | Click the outline                                  |
| **Fold**  | Where the fabric folds                                        | Click two points of the outline                    |
| **Grain** | The direction of the fabric's threads, which changes the hang | Drag across the piece along the grain              |
