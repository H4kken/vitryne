Vitryne lets indie creators and clothing brands design garments before anything is cut: draw
the pattern pieces, sew them together, and drape them on a body in real time. The cloth
simulation runs on your graphics card, in the browser, through WebGPU.

## What you can do

- **Draw patterns:** pen and ready shapes, typed lengths, curved edges, notches, grain lines.
- **Sew:** pick whole edges to join, or take the suggested seams.
- **Place and drape:** put pieces around the body, then pinch and pull the cloth.
- **Choose fabrics:** physical presets such as denim and fleece, per piece or for the garment.
- **Export:** glTF and OBJ for 3D tools.
