## Introduction

**Vitryne is a free and open-source Clothes Modeling Software** that runs in your browser.
It's an alternative to traditional 3D modeling software (like Browzwear, CLO, Style3D or Optitex), specifically designed for fashion designers and clothing enthusiasts.

## Quick start

Go to [vitryne.app](https://vitryne.app) and start from an example or a blank pattern. Your work is kept as one draft and saves by
itself. Create a free account to keep every project, open them on any device, and export.

## What you can do

- **Draw patterns:** pen and ready shapes, typed lengths, curved edges, notches, grain lines.
- **Sew:** pick whole edges to join, or take the suggested seams.
- **Place and drape:** put pieces around the body, then pinch and pull the cloth.
- **Choose fabrics:** physical presets such as denim and fleece, per piece or for the garment.
- **Export:** glTF and OBJ for 3D tools.
