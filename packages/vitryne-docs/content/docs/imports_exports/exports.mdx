Exporting needs a **free account**: without one, Vitryne asks you to create it first. Your
projects stay saved in your account either way; an export is a copy you keep.

## Export a garment

1. Let the garment drape until it looks right, then pause it (space bar): the export takes the
   fabric **where it is now**.
2. Choose **Export** in the bar at the top.
3. Pick a format and choose **Export**.

| Format               | What you get                                     | Open it in                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| **glTF 2.0 (.glb)**  | One file: the garment, its fabrics and colours   | Blender, most 3D viewers and web tools |
| **Wavefront (.obj)** | Two files: `.obj` (the shape) and `.mtl` (looks) | Almost any 3D software                 |
| **Project (.vtry)**  | The whole project                                | Vitryne                                |

glTF is the one to choose unless your software asks for OBJ.

## What's in a 3D export

- **The garment as it hangs**: one object, each piece a separate part with its fabric as the
  material.
- **Your pattern as the texture layout:** an image or print maps onto each piece the way it would
  be printed on the flat fabric before cutting.
- **The body**, if you turn on **Include avatar**: a separate object, handy to show the garment
  on something.

Sizes are in **metres**, with up being up (+Y) and the body facing you (+Z), so the garment comes
in at real size.

## Keep a copy of the project

**Project (.vtry)** saves everything: pieces, seams, fabrics, the body and the view. Use it to
keep a backup, or to send a design to someone who uses Vitryne: they open it with **Import a
file** on the start screen, and it becomes a new project of theirs.

You can also export a project without opening it: on the start screen, open the **⋯** menu on
its card and choose **Export (.vtry)**.

<Callout title="Patterns for cutting">
  Exporting the pattern itself (a DXF to print or cut from) will come once
  Vitryne can add seam allowance: a pattern without it isn't ready to cut.
</Callout>
