Vitryne works with the files you already have: patterns made elsewhere come in, and your draped
garment goes out to 3D software.

<Cards>
  <Card icon="Upload" title="Imports" href="/imports_exports/imports">
    Bring in a pattern from your pattern software, or a Vitryne project file.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="Download" title="Exports" href="/imports_exports/exports">
    Take your draped garment to 3D software, or keep a copy of the whole
    project.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Exporting needs a free account. Your projects themselves are saved as you work, so there is nothing to
export just to keep your work.
