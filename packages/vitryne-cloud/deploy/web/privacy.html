Without the cloud, nothing leaves your browser

Vitryne runs in your browser. Opening examples, importing patterns, designing, saving to your computer and exporting send nothing to us: your patterns and garments stay on your device. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookie. While you work, an unsaved copy of your project is kept in your browser's storage so a crash loses nothing; it never leaves your device.

When you save to the cloud

Only if you choose Save to the cloud or sign in, we store:

What Why How long
Your projects (the pattern pieces, seams, fabrics and avatar in them, and their names) To keep them for you and open them on your devices Until you delete them or your account; without an account, 30 days after your last visit
An account identifier, created the first time you save to the cloud To know which projects are yours Until you delete your account; without an account, 30 days after your last visit
Your email address and a password (stored only as a hash), if you create an account; or, if you sign in with Google, the email address and name Google shares To let you sign in and open your projects elsewhere, and to send you the codes that confirm your address or reset your password Until you delete your account
Sign-in records: when you signed in, and the IP address used To keep accounts secure and to limit abuse (for example, how many new accounts one network creates per hour) As long as the account exists

Your browser keeps your sign-in session in its local storage, which is needed for the cloud features you asked for. How much each person can store is shown in their account (the Cloud button).

We use this data only to provide cloud storage to you (the legal basis is our agreement with you to provide that service, GDPR art. 6(1)(b), and, for the sign-in records, our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, art. 6(1)(f)). We don't sell it, don't use it for advertising, and don't count or profile visitors.

Your rights

  • See and take your data: in Vitryne, open Cloud and choose Download all my data: every project as a file, and what your account holds.
  • Delete it: Cloud, then Delete my account removes your account, your projects and their files at once.
  • Correct it, restrict or object to its use: write to {{if env "OPERATOR_CONTACT"}}{{env "OPERATOR_CONTACT"}}{{else}}the operator{{end}}.
  • Complain: you can contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL, cnil.fr).
